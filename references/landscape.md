# 风景画法

先确定来源。A、B、C 都是成品透明素材，普通拼接直接复用。用户点名局部改动时只调整风景图层。定制风景用用户提供的实景图片；用户提出定制但尚未提供地点照片时，先索取该照片。

| 预设 | 识别与适配 |
| --- | --- |
| A 湖畔博雅塔 | 湖对岸细长塔身，塔檐浅、密。树用疏落柳线和少量浅橄榄绿。保留倒影时让它与塔同轴，用断续横向水纹提示倒置轮廓，避免深色整块倒影。 |
| B 秋叶博雅塔 | 保留略侧看的塔形。黄绿色阔叶树用留白和分散色点归纳。几片枫叶以各自的叶柄连到枝条，大小和朝向错落，橙黄集中在叶中，留出叶尖。水面只画几道淡横纹。 |
| C 未名湖石 | 高窄直立的不规则石体，少量灰色平涂。石面红字从上到下为“未”“名”“湖”，保持字形与方向，拼接时不要镜像。 |

## 定制风景提示词

实景照决定地标轮廓、比例、视角、相邻物体的相对位置及原有题字。人物稿或 A/B/C 之一决定画法。先从照片挑一处识别重点，其余用少量线条压缩。按有无装饰规划风景高度。有装饰时，风景主体从成品底边排到中线附近。无装饰时，可把自然延伸的树冠、地标或山形画得更高。两种情况下风景的线条和色块都应比人物轻。海港、花丛等原本横向的场景，可取较窄的一段，利用前景水面或花叶与后景主体的高低关系形成纵向层次。树冠、建筑或最高的花簇应构成上沿，零散云朵和透明边距不计作主体高度。按竖幅里实际露出的部分规划识别重点。景物可向画外延伸，人物也可以盖住较大一部分。

```text
Draw one isolated compact landscape spot illustration for a hand-drawn portrait.
[Location photo] is the physical reference. Preserve its recognizable landmark silhouette, proportions, viewpoint, and a few important spatial relationships. Keep any requested visible landmark inscription in its original reading direction and exact wording.
[Portrait or a preset sketch] is a drawing-technique reference only. Match its slightly variable warm-black pen pressure and economical, continuous structural contours. Make the landscape ink lighter and sparser than the portrait. Simplify [landmark] with a few decisive contours, omit fine masonry or surface detail. Indicate [surroundings] with open loose silhouettes, two or three strokes per trunk or branch, and widely separated marker dabs.
Arrange the main scene from a continuous visible ground or water base up to recognizable trees, buildings, or blooms. Give it natural vertical structure to fit the selected poster layout: [with decorations, rise from the base to about mid-canvas; without decorations, rise from the base to mid-canvas or higher as the composition calls for]. For a wide scene, show a narrower viewpoint with a visible foreground and a higher focal feature. Use no disconnected elements to pad the visible height.
Use flat water-based marker passes, irregular ends, and large areas of paper-white or open space. Use a small quiet palette drawn from [pale ochre, muted olive, cool gray, or colors appropriate to the photo]. Keep most foliage unfilled. If there is water, use a few short irregular horizontal ripples; include only an abbreviated reflection when the photo or user calls for it. Avoid photographic shading, gradients, solid foliage masses, dense hatching, or full-page landscape detail.
Export a true transparent PNG, with open sky and surroundings transparent and deliberate pale fills inside the landmark opaque. Keep continuous forms in the source layer so trees, branches, water or buildings can run behind the portrait or be clipped at the poster border during assembly. Place the recognizable part of the scene where it will remain visible. No figures, extra lettering, border, paper rectangle or painted checkerboard.
```

题字看不清时请用户提供准确文字，或先出无字风景并预留位置。输出后对照实景核验轮廓与视角，再对照人物稿核验笔触和颜色浓淡。
